Today's goal: Use keyboard shortcuts instead of the mouse.
Step 1Learn the moves

Hold down Ctrl with one finger, then tap the letter. Let go of both keys. On a Mac, use ⌘ Command instead of Ctrl. Bronze members learn the first 5. Silver and Gold members learn all 10.

Step 2Play: Shortcut Challenge

Question 1 of 10Streak: 0

Press the shortcut to…

COPY

Step 3Use it for real: Practice Pad

Click inside the Practice Pad. Do each task using only the keyboard. Each task ticks itself when you do it.

  • ✓Select all the text (Ctrl + A)
  • ✓Copy it (Ctrl + C)
  • ✓Click at the end and paste it (Ctrl + V)
  • ✓Oops! Undo the paste (Ctrl + Z)
  • ✓Highlight one word and cut it (Ctrl + X)
  • ✓Bring it back with Undo, then Redo (Ctrl + Y)
Step 4Show & Share
  • Bronze: Show your stars. Which shortcut was the easiest to remember?
  • Silver: Teach a friend one shortcut he or she did not know.
  • Gold: Tell the club your best speed score. Can anyone beat it next week?
🧑‍🏫 Teacher notes
  • Timing (25 min): Step 1 · 5 min together on the projector · Step 2 · 10 min · Step 3 · 7 min · Step 4 in Show & Share.
  • Levels: most Standard 4 members start at Bronze; many Standard 6 members can go straight to Silver. Move up after 3 stars.
  • Badge (automatic): Bronze or Silver with 2+ stars, or Gold with 12+ correct → 🥷 Shortcut Ninja. Every finished game is one row in Mission Results.
  • Common mistake: pressing the letter first, then Ctrl. The game says "Hold Ctrl first".
  • Browser note: students click on the page before playing so it can hear the keyboard. The Practice Pad is not recorded.
