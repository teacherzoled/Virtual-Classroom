Sentence starter: “To stay safe online, I should… because…”

🙂 Personal information

Information about you that cannot be used to find out exactly who you are, because many people share it. Example: your favourite food.

🔒 Private information

Information about you that can be used to find out exactly who you are, because it belongs only to you. Example: your home address.

🦸 Upstander

A person who supports and stands up for someone who is being treated badly, online or in person.

🗂 Sort it! Personal or private?

Choose for each piece of information. Then press Check my sorting.

💡 How Sprite Lab is different

In Sprite Lab, all the code under when run happens right away, in order, as soon as you press Run. Code inside an event block (like when clicked) waits until that event happens.
