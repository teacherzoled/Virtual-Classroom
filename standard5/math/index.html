📐 Mathematics

30 teaching weeks  ·  4 cycles  ·  school weeks 4–33

🔢
Welcome, Standard 5! Standard 5 is the bridge year — it takes what you learned in Standard 4 and gets you ready for Standard 6. Everything you need is here: your weekly lessons, your daily Power-Ups, every graded assessment, and the tools you can reach for whenever you get stuck.
🧭
How the year is built. School weeks 1–3 are the Diagnostic and Repair block. Teaching runs from week 4 to week 33 across four cycles — 150 lessons in all. Weeks 34–36 are buffer for national assessments and school activities. Each card below opens one teaching week.

Simulations  ·  play until the idea clicks

⭕
Pi Discovery Lab
Measure the circumference and diameter of circle after circle, record the ratio, and watch every answer settle near the same number — π before it is ever given to you
MA 6.35 Discovery Practice only
📦
Net Folding Lab
Fold a flat net into a solid and unfold it again. Test which nets actually close, then compare the surface area you can see with the volume you cannot
MA 6.36–6.37 3D Practice only
🪞
Coordinate Reflection Grid
Plot a shape, reflect it in the x-axis or y-axis, and read what happens to each coordinate. Then build a tessellation from the result
MA 6.38–6.39 Geometry Practice only
🎲
Probability Simulator
Roll, spin or draw a hundred times, a thousand times, ten thousand times — and watch the experimental result close in on the theoretical one
MA 9.21–9.22 Discovery Practice only
📊
Graph Builder
Enter your own data and build a bar graph, line graph or circle graph from it — then see which graph tells the story best
MA 9.19–9.20 Data Practice only
🌳
Factor Tree Builder
